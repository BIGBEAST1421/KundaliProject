# AstroTrue — Product Context

## Register
product (with a brand-register landing page at `/`)

## Platform
web

## Users
People in India and the diaspora (20–45) who want a Vedic birth-chart reading or a marriage-compatibility check for themselves or family — mostly non-technical, often on a phone, frequently sharing the result with parents or a partner.

## Purpose
Turn a birth date/time/place into an honest, easy-to-read Vedic report (chart + AI insights) and a clear couple-compatibility report, each with a permanent shareable link and a printable version.

## Positioning
Real Swiss-Ephemeris math and classical Ashta Koota scoring, explained in plain language — balanced (strengths *and* genuine concerns), never fear-mongering, never generic horoscope filler.

## Brand personality
Calm, trustworthy, warm, precise. A well-read family astrologer who explains things clearly — not a fortune-teller, not a tech demo.

## Anti-references
Neon-purple "cosmic" gradients, glassmorphism, navy-and-gold mysticism, gamified effects, horoscope-app clutter, robotic AI-report tone.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-navigable forms, reduced-motion alternatives for all loaders, Hindi (Devanagari) UI parity.

## Design principles
1. Insights first — the reading is the product; chrome stays quiet.
2. Balance is visible — strengths and concerns are equally discoverable.
3. One accent, used for meaning (actions, current dasha), not decoration.
4. Every wait is contextual — loaders say what is happening in astrology terms.
